    Process

    How we work

    Desanya drafts the work. You approve before anything goes live. Clear packages, no surprises — and every lead comes to you to close.

    Steps

    1. 01

      Intro chat

      A short, no-pressure conversation about what you’re building, who it’s for, and what done looks like.

    2. 02

      Package or quote

      A published package price — or a custom quote for bigger scopes — in writing, before any work starts.

    3. 03

      Desanya drafts

      Site, copy, blog, social, or SEO — prepared for your review, in your voice, aimed at your goals.

    4. 04

      You approve

      Nothing goes live without your sign-off. Feedback happens here — not after launch.

    5. 05

      Launch

      Your site, posts, or listings go live. Leads come straight to you.

    6. 06

      Ongoing care optional

      Add monthly care ($99/mo), SEO & Google managing ($450/mo), social managing ($850/mo), or blog posts ($700/mo) when you want ongoing support.

    Ground rules

    What you can count on

    Clear prices

    Packages are published on the services page. Custom work is quoted up front.

    Your approval first

    Drafts come to you. You decide what ships — and when.

    One point of contact

    You work directly with Desanya Studio — no hand-offs, no strangers.

    You own the leads

    Inquiries from your site go to you to close.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need to know which package I want?

    No. Start with an intro chat — you’ll get a straight recommendation, even if that’s “you only need the $400 content package.”

    What if my project doesn’t fit a package?

    Larger builds, branding, and illustration are quoted per project. Use the contact form and choose “Custom / other.”

    Will anything publish without my approval?

    Never. Every site change, post, and listing update is drafted for your sign-off first.

    Can I add ongoing help later?

    Yes. Monthly care, SEO & Google managing, social managing, AI managing, and monthly blog posts can be added any time after launch.
