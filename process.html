Process

How engagements work

I draft the work (site, copy, blog, social, SEO). You approve before anything goes live or gets sent. Clear packages, no surprises — and every lead from chat comes to you to close.

Steps

  1. 01

    Intro chat

    A short, no-pressure conversation about what you’re building, who it’s for, and what “done” looks like.

  2. 02

    Package or quote

    You get a published package price — or a custom quote for bigger scopes — in writing, before any work starts.

  3. 03

    I draft the work

    Site, copy, blog posts, social, or SEO — drafted for your review, in your voice, with your goals in mind.

  4. 04

    You approve

    Nothing goes live or gets sent without your sign-off. Feedback rounds happen here, not after launch.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Your site, posts, or listings go live. Leads come straight to you to close.

  6. 06

    Ongoing care optional

    Monthly care ($99/mo), SEO & Google managing ($450/mo), social managing ($850/mo), or blog posts ($700/mo) keep things fresh.

Ground rules

What you can count on

Clear prices

Packages are published on the services page. Anything custom is quoted up front.

Your approval first

Drafts come to you. You decide what ships, and when.

One point of contact

You work directly with Desanya Studio — no hand-offs between strangers.

You own the leads

Inquiries from your site and chat go to you to close.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to know what package I want?

No. Start with an intro chat — you’ll get a straight recommendation, even if that’s “you only need the $400 content package.”

What if my project doesn’t fit a package?

Larger builds, branding bundles, and illustration are quoted per project. Use the contact form and pick “Custom / other.”

Will anything be published without me seeing it?

Never. Every site change, post, and listing update is drafted for your approval first.

Can I add ongoing help later?

Yes. Monthly care, SEO & Google managing, social managing, and monthly blog posts can be added any time after launch.
