    Sherman’s Shih Tzu Puppies ↗ (opens in a new tab)

    A family breeder of AKC and CKC Shih Tzus in Niota, East Tennessee, serving all of Tennessee.

    Before

    Grinding it out at the Sweetwater Flea Market, puppies went for $400–$500. After booth fees, gas, food, and shots, there was barely a profit left.

    Then COVID closed the market for good. It was a moment that could have ended it.

    What we built

    Desanya Studio’s first build was a real website, and a Google Business Profile to go with it.

    After

    $1,500–$2,500
    a puppy, up from $400–$500
    4.8★
    from 23 Google reviews
    2,186
    Google profile interactions in six months

    Today, families drive in from all over the US. They don’t ship. From May through October 2026, Google’s Performance dashboard recorded those 2,186 interactions — calls, chats, directions, and more.

    The business grew enough to build the dogs a two-story tiny home.

    Ongoing care

    Desanya fully manages the website and the verified Google Business Profile. Regular “What’s new” puppy updates go up there, such as “Brindle AKC male” and “AKC imperial male.”

    Your business can have that same kind of care: a website, a Google Business Profile, and Desanya Studio to manage both.
